Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 1, 2026

Welcome to Undo. Your privacy, peace of mind, and control over your personal data are at the core of everything we build. Undo is designed from the ground up as an ephemeral, privacy-first journaling and thought-purging application.

1. Core Privacy Philosophy: Ephemeral & Local-First

Undo is built on the principle of ephemeral data management:

  • Local Storage First: All your personal thoughts, notes, and journal entries are stored locally on your device.
  • Auto-Purging: By design, thoughts auto-purge based on your selected timer settings (1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, or custom durations). Purged data is permanently removed.
  • Zero Permanent Cloud Backups: Undo does not maintain a central server database storing your unencrypted thoughts or personal journals.

2. Information We Process

A. Local User Data: Journal entries and preferences stored locally on device.

B. AI Insights (Google Gemini): Optional text prompts sent securely for real-time analysis, not retained or stored by Undo.

C. Telemetry & Edge Analytics (Supabase): Anonymous crash logs and app version telemetry routed via Supabase Edge Functions on mobile apps.

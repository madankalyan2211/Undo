# Privacy Policy for Undo

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026  

Welcome to **Undo** ("we," "our," or "us"). Your privacy, peace of mind, and control over your personal data are at the core of everything we build. Undo is designed from the ground up as an ephemeral, privacy-first journaling and thought-purging application.

---

## 1. Core Privacy Philosophy: Ephemeral & Local-First

Undo is built on the principle of **ephemeral data management**:
- **Local Storage First:** All your personal thoughts, notes, and journal entries are created and stored locally on your device using local storage or native device storage.
- **Auto-Purging:** By design, your thoughts auto-purge based on your selected timer settings (1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, or custom durations). Once purged, this data is permanently erased from your local device storage.
- **Zero Permanent Cloud Backups:** Undo does not maintain a central server database storing your unencrypted thoughts or personal journals.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### A. Local User Data
- **Journal Entries & Reflections:** Created locally on your device. Never transmitted to our servers for storage.
- **App Preferences:** Settings such as timer durations, theme preferences, and lock settings are stored locally on your device.

### B. AI Insights & Processing (Google Gemini)
- If you request AI emotional insights or summaries within the app, the relevant text prompt is securely sent to Google's **Gemini AI API** to generate real-time feedback.
- Data sent to Gemini is used strictly to generate your requested response and is **not retained or stored** by Undo.

### C. Application Telemetry & Edge Analytics (Supabase)
- On mobile devices, non-identifiable telemetry (such as crash reports, anonymous feature usage, and app version metrics) is routed through secure **Supabase Edge Functions**.
- No personal journal entries, raw text thoughts, or personally identifiable information (PII) are ever included in analytics payloads.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

We process minimal data exclusively to:
1. Provide core application functionalities (writing, purging, saved items, lock features).
2. Generate real-time AI insights when requested by you.
3. Diagnose app crashes and improve system stability via anonymized analytics.

## 5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard practices to protect your data:
- **Local Device Protection:** Your data remains on your physical device under your control.
- **Security Lock Feature:** You can enable the built-in security passcode feature within the app settings to restrict access on your device.
- **HTTPS/TLS Encryption:** All communications with third-party APIs (Gemini and Supabase) are encrypted in transit using standard TLS protocols.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Data Control

Because your data is stored locally on your device:
- **Access & Viewing:** You can view all saved and active entries directly inside the app.
- **Instant Deletion / Purging:** You can manually purge or delete your thoughts at any time.
- **App Reset:** Clearing your browser cache or uninstalling the app permanently erases all local Undo data.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Undo is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.

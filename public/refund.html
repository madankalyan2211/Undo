Undo Plus is a digital subscription service. Upon successful payment verification via Razorpay, your account is immediately upgraded to unlock all sensory rituals, soundscapes, ASMR packs, and focus intention features.

2. Refund Window (7-Day Guarantee)

We want you to be completely at peace with your sanctuary. If you are unsatisfied with your Undo Plus purchase for any reason, you may request a 100% full refund within 7 days of your initial purchase date.

3. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, please email our support team at support@undo.app with:

  • Your registered Google account email.

4. Refund Processing SLA

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment source (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or NetBanking) within 5 to 7 business days as per standard banking partner processing times.

5. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel any recurring subscription at any time directly through the app or by writing to support. Your access will remain active until the end of your current paid billing period.
